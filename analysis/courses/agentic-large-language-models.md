# Book: Agentic Large Language Models

A broad book that connects language-model foundations and training to reasoning, tool use, retrieval, applications, multi-agent behavior, and cognitive and mechanistic analysis. It is reported separately from the seven courses.

## Scope and limits

- All 23 archived chapter conversions were screened by their section structure.
- The book is one source, not 23 courses. Full HTML and PDF copies are not counted as separate sources.
- Book-only topics have zero course frequency until supported by a course. An asterisk on an advanced chapter is not treated as missing content; those chapters contain full text.
- Named applications and methods are grouped at a stated scope. The bibliography does not create topic coverage by itself.
