# Stanford: Engineering AI Agents

An engineering course that connects agent components, design patterns, data, optimization, and evaluation. Available slides support the completed lectures. Later safety and personal-agent topics appear in the schedule and are kept separate.

[Archived source](../../courses/stanford-cs329z/README.md) · [Shared dictionary](../DICTIONARY.md) · [Summary](../README.md)

## Scope and limits

- Fourteen slide decks are available. Later scheduled lectures have no archived slide deck.
- Core evidence comes from lecture slides. A schedule-only topic is marked planned, even when a related reading is archived.
- The inventory separates general topics, specific patterns, methods, tools, and applications. Repeated slides do not increase course frequency.
