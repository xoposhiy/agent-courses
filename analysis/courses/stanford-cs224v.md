# Stanford: Agentic AI

A course on dependable agents for knowledge work. It emphasizes evidence, formal decisions, clinical matching, structured and unstructured data, research workflows, dialogue specifications, document transcription, and database reconciliation.

[Archived source](../../courses/stanford-cs224v/README.md) · [Shared dictionary](../DICTIONARY.md) · [Summary](../README.md)

## Scope and limits

- Eleven slide decks and two homework descriptions are available.
- Thousands of saved traces, input records, and vendored code files do not increase frequency.
- The future context-management and harness-behavior sessions are schedule-only. A topic already supported by an available lecture or assignment is counted as taught.
- Clinical examples are analyzed as course content, not as medical guidance.
