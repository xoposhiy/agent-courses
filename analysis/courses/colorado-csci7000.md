# CU Boulder: Building AI Agents

A graduate seminar on agentic software engineering. The main evidence is the assigned research literature, supported by one introduction deck and project instructions. It emphasizes tool use, coding agents, multi-agent work, safety, harness improvement, and the limits of benchmarks.

[Archived source](../../courses/colorado-csci7000/README.md) · [Shared dictionary](../DICTIONARY.md) · [Summary](../README.md)

## Scope and limits

- Only the introductory slide deck is archived. Most substantive topics are marked assigned, not taught from lecture evidence.
- The MCP downstream-failure paper and the agentic-refactoring paper are unavailable. Their scheduled topics are not counted as confirmed from those papers.
- Survey readings support broad architecture topics. Their bibliographies do not establish additional course coverage.
- The Kiro IDE sessions occur after the archive date and have no teaching materials in the archive.
