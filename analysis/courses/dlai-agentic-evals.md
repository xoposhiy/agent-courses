# DeepLearning.AI / Arize: Evaluating and Improving Agentic Applications

A focused evaluation course with a complete cycle from requirements and trace inspection to test sets, evaluators, action and outcome checks, controlled changes, and production regression tests.

[Archived source](../../courses/dlai-agentic-evals/README.md) · [Shared dictionary](../DICTIONARY.md) · [Summary](../README.md)

## Scope and limits

- Five slide decks and 28 lesson transcripts are available.
- Restricted labs and quizzes are not reconstructed or counted as available teaching content.
- Improvement-test planning is evaluation work; it is not counted as agent task planning. A lesson about a judge is evaluation, while a persona exercise in another course is not automatically LLM-as-judge training.
