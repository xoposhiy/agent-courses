# Models & Pricing

Model prices are listed in units of per 1M tokens. A token, the smallest unit of text that the model recognizes, can be a word, a number, or even a punctuation mark. We bill model usage based on the total number of input and output tokens by the model.

## Web Search & Fetch Pricing[​](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing/#web-tools-pricing "Direct link to Web Search & Fetch Pricing")

Search (text, image) and web fetch are billed per use, whether you call the [Search and Fetch APIs](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/guides/search) directly or the model uses them through tools such as the [web tools](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/guides/web_tools).

| Item | Unit | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search (text, image) | Per 1,000 searches | $0.75 |
| Web fetch | Per 1,000 fetches | $0.75 |

For the billing rules, see [Search & Fetch](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/guides/search) and [Web Tools](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/guides/web_tools#billing).

## Deduction Rules[​](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing/#deduction-rules "Direct link to Deduction Rules")

For model usage, the expense = number of tokens × price. Search (text, image) and web fetch are charged per use at the rates above.
The corresponding fees will be directly deducted from your topped-up balance or granted balance, with a preference for using the granted balance first when both balances are available.

Product prices may vary and DeepSeek reserves the right to adjust them. We recommend topping up based on your actual usage and regularly checking this page for the most recent pricing information.
