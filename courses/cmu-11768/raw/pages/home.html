Course Overview

This course studies LLM-based AI agents: systems that use large language models to perceive, reason, plan, and act over many steps. Agents interact with their environments — calling tools, navigating interfaces, writing code, working alongside people — to complete real tasks.

The course covers both the foundations of building agents and active research questions around long-horizon reasoning, training, safety, and interaction. We emphasize hands-on implementation with modern frameworks and the training methods that make agents more capable and reliable.
