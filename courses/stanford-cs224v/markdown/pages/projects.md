## Project Info

**Each project will be in a group of 2 students.**

A core part of CS 224V is a project you work on throughout the quarter. We have mentors and advisors across many disciplines who have signed on to help you with these projects, from Stanford and from external partners. **All projects are mentored and supervised on a weekly basis.**

[The project proposals document](../projects/project-proposals.md) is the starting point for choosing a topic. **You are most welcome to propose your own project** — please post it in that document so it can attract partners.
