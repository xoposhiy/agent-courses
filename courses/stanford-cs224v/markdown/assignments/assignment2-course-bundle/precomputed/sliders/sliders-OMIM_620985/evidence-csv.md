### Discriminating Findings and Differential Diagnosis

Clinical differentiation of Karayol-Borroto-Haghshenas syndrome based solely on standard neurodevelopmental symptoms is challenging due to phenotypic overlap across Mendelian neurodevelopmental disorders F16. However, when contrasted against its closest biochemical and epigenetic counterparts within the male-specific lethal (MSL) chromatin-modifying complex, several distinctive patterns emerge:

1. **High Prevalence of Autism Spectrum Behaviors (~64%)**: Autistic traits and social-communication deficits occur in approximately 64.3% of individuals with *MSL2* mutations (R Karayol et al., 2024, [PMID:38815585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38815585); S Luo et al., 2026, [DOI:10.1007/s00018-026-06237-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00018-026-06237-7)) F6, F7. This behavioral prevalence is distinctly higher than in *MSL3*-related Basilicata-Akhtar syndrome (OMIM #300271; global patient count ~40–42) and *KAT8*-related disorders, providing a key neurobehavioral divergence (V Kulkarni et al., 2024, Cureus; Y Qing et al., 2025, [DOI:10.21037/tp-2025-227](https://doi.org/10.21037/tp-2025-227)) F2, F7.
2. **Qualitative Neuromotor Deficit Profile**: Motor impairment in *MSL2* is dominated by early feeding difficulties (81.3%), axial/truncal hypotonia (76.5%), and progressive motor incoordination or gait ataxia (11/25, 44%) rather than early-onset refractory epileptic encephalopathy or progressive hypertonic spasticity (R Karayol et al., 2024, [PMID:38815585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38815585)) F6, F8, F10. In contrast, severe *KAT8* mutations and severe presentations of Basilicata-Akhtar syndrome feature intractable seizures and spastic quadriplegia (Z Wang et al., 2026, iScience, [S2589-0042(26)02056-0](https://www.cell.com/iscience/fulltext/S2589-0042(26)02056-0)) F8.
3. **Confusable Chromatinopathies and Molecular Episignature Resolution**: The clinical presentation of *MSL2* exhibits non-specific features common across epigenetic conditions (e.g., global developmental delay, intellectual disability, hypotonia, and varied facial dysmorphism) (X Lu et al., 2024, [DOI:10.1038/s41431-024-01576-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41431-024-01576-0)) F16. Bedside phenotypic distinction alone between *MSL2* and allied epigenetic entities offers **low-to-moderate clinical confidence** F16. Definitive distinction relies on a high-dimensional peripheral blood DNA methylation episignature, which achieves near-perfect classification (Methylation Variant Pathogenicity [MVP] score approaching 1.0) against controls, *MSL3*-related Basilicata-Akhtar syndrome, and *KAT8*-related disorders (R Karayol et al., 2024, [DOI:10.1016/j.ajhg.2024.05.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2024.05.001)) F4.
