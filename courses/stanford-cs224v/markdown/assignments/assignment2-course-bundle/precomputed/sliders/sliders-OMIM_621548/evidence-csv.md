Retinitis pigmentosa 101 (RP101; OMIM #621548) is an autosomal recessive retinal dystrophy caused by biallelic pathogenic variants in *CLN3*, a gene localized to chromosome 16p12.1 encoding the 438-amino acid lysosomal/endosomal transmembrane protein battenin (F1, F6).
