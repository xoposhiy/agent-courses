## Executive Summary & Feasibility of Phenotype-Only Identification

Neurodevelopmental disorder with early-onset seizures, facial dysmorphism, and behavioral abnormalities (NEDSFBA, OMIM #621390) is an autosomal dominant condition caused by heterozygous de novo missense variants in the *KLHL20* gene, located on chromosome 1q25 F1, F6. *KLHL20* encodes Kelch-like protein 20, a substrate adaptor for Cullin3 (CUL3)-RING E3 ubiquitin ligases that directs ubiquitination and plays critical roles in neurite outgrowth and synaptic homeostasis F5.

From clinical phenotypes alone, **a clinician cannot definitively identify an affected individual out of a group of patients with overlapping developmental, epileptic, or behavioral features without molecular testing** (F3; F4; F8). Although early-onset seizures, intellectual disability, and behavioral disturbances (autism spectrum disorder, hyperactivity) occur with high frequency, the craniofacial dysmorphic features are subtle and variable, lacking an identifiable syndromic ""gestalt"" (F3; F4).
