About

Who we are and what we’re working on

Big Local News, based at Stanford University, helps newsrooms do important accountability journalism without the need for their own data specialists or extensive programming knowledge. We gather data to investigate patterns and detect newsworthy events, and provide datasets, tools, and training free to any newsroom. We also collaborate with local journalists to turn interesting datasets into multi-newsroom reporting projects.

The biglocalnews.org site offers a free archiving service for journalists to store, share, and publish data. Selected projects are preserved by the Stanford Digital Repository. Our Industrial Affiliates program offers specialized services for more sophisticated data work through an annual membership.
