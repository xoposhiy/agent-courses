[02:58] Now, here's where this gets especially useful.

[03:00] An outcome evaluator and a trajectory evaluator can look at the exact

[03:07] Take the Northwind refund case. The customer asks for a

[03:31] before it verified the customer.

[03:33] only after the refund had already been issued did it retrieve

[03:37] So now you have two pieces of evidence that tell you different things.

[03:41] The outcome check says yes, the refund exists

[03:45] The identity before refund check says no, the agent didn't follow the

[03:53] The outcome tells you that the agent produced the intended state,

[04:00] And that distinction matters because a successful outcome doesn't
