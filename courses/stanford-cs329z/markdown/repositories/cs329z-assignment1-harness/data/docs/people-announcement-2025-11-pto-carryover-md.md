# Announcement: PTO Carryover Limit Raised

Posted: 2025-11-17
From: Tessa Nguyen, Director of People Operations
To: All US employees

Many of you told us in the October survey that the end of the year is a hard time to use up vacation days, because customer go-lives and the winter closure fall in the same weeks. We have changed the carryover rule in response.

## What is changing

Starting with the rollover from 2025 into 2026, you may carry over up to 10 days (80 hours) of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Carried-over PTO must be used by June 30 of that year.

This replaces Section 5 of the Paid Time Off Policy (HR-101, effective 2025-01-01), which allowed 5 days and set a deadline of March 31. The same section of the Employee Handbook is replaced as well. The policy document will be reissued at its next annual review. Until then, this announcement is the rule.

## What is not changing

- Accrual rates stay the same.
- PTO above the carryover limit is still forfeited on December 31, except where state law does not permit forfeiture.
- Sick leave and floating holidays still do not carry over.
- The Netherlands addendum is not affected.

## What you need to do

Nothing. PeopleHub will apply the new limit automatically on December 31, 2025. You can see your projected year-end balance under Time Off, Balances.
